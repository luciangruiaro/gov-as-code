# ADR backlog

The questions that need a decision, before they become ADRs. An entry is `open` until someone works on it, `partial` while its ADR is a draft or waits on an open item, and `decided` once its ADR is ratified. New entries come from alignment worksheets (a PRD change that needs a decision), brainstorms, reviews and the team's proposals on the shared drive (`Team/Proposals/`).
