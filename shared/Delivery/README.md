# Delivery

**Holds:** the delivery planner (milestones, phases, capacity), scope and estimate sheets, the risk and issue log, and status reports.

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `Delivery planner.<xlsx\|gsheet>` | Milestones, phases, owners (roles), dates |
| `Risks and issues.<xlsx\|gsheet>` | The RAID log |
| [`Updates/`](Updates/README.md) | Weekly status reports, generated or written: `weekly-report-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf` |

**Written by:** delivery lead, and the weekly report tool for `Updates/`. **Read by:** the team, the client, the daily digest (milestone dates).

**Rules:** contract (SOW) documents stay in the vendor's contract system, not here; cite their sections instead.
